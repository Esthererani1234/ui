# Gold On The Spot tax preview

## What customers can try

Open `/tax-preview` on the preview deployment to search all 50 states and Washington, DC, read the government guidance, and try sample orders for five minimums and six taxable destinations. A qualifying New York investment bar priced at exactly $1,000 needs another cent under the example store minimum. The two Washington public addresses share ZIP 98501. For Q4 2026, a taxable $20,000 purchase calculates $2,000 at the Olympia address and $1,960 at the Tumwater address. These examples use fictional transactions and never reserve inventory or initiate payment.

Checkout keeps all 50 states available for shipping. It displays a minimum notice only when the eligible order is short. The store floors are $1,000.01 for New York and Maryland, $2,000 for California, and $1,000 for Massachusetts and Connecticut. Connecticut's current floor ends on June 30, 2027. New Jersey has a product-specific coin test. A positive customer charge appears as one **Tax** line. Zero, exempt, pending and noncollection statuses have no customer tax row. Failed verification is handled before payment; it never produces an assumed zero. New York investment purchasers confirm their purchase purpose in the existing agreement.

## Delivery address correction

The cart, checkout and account address editor use a nationwide street lookup. A street plus ZIP can fill missing city and state. Equivalent street abbreviations are standardized and unit information is preserved. Customers confirm changes to existing names or ZIP codes. Competing matches or different house numbers require an edit. Payments, the order, a saved default address and the tax assessment use the same confirmed destination. Pickup uses the fixed office address.

The preview at `/tax-preview#delivery-address` allows a read-only test using the [US Census Geocoder](https://www.census.gov/data/developers/data-sets/Geocoding-services.html). Its street ranges establish a geographic match. Apartment occupancy and carrier deliverability are outside that coverage. In an authorized USPS shipping integration, `USPS_ADDRESS_ENABLED=true` with server-only `USPS_CLIENT_ID` and `USPS_CLIENT_SECRET` selects [USPS Addresses](https://developers.usps.com/addressesv3) for delivery-point confirmation. The preview uses Census and has no USPS credentials. The system never treats an unanswered lookup as a successful postal verification.

## Checkout and accounting

- Delivery uses a full street address. Pickup uses the store’s New York address. Cart estimates and final checkout use the same calculation service. Discounts are allocated across merchandise, and reviewed rules determine how shipping and payment charges affect the taxable base.
- The server binds the assessment to the customer, price lock, payment method, fulfillment, address and rule revision. The order stores the assessment with the calculated total. Missing or expired approvals block an affected checkout.
- The calculation supports separate qualifying merchandise, voluntary store minimums, price-to-metal-value limits and individual coin tests. Government rate components and seller-level gross receipts or excise charges have separate ledger fields.
- Admin → Sales Tax & Compliance provides state research, registrations, source and product review, private evidence, nexus alerts, administrative filing schedules, payment confirmations, and a period ledger CSV. Refund entries require a confirmed reference and are capped by the original tax components.
- A filing or remittance enters the ledger only after an administrator records the actual event. Reconcile earlier orders, other selling channels, refunds and tax payments against business records before using totals for returns.

## Local rate and registration gaps

Washington cross-checks its Department of Revenue street-address response against the effective quarterly location rate and official street number range, street side and ZIP. The Q4 2026 download contains 1,229,719 address rows. Ambiguous, stale, conflicting, and ZIP-only matches require review. The preview includes two real benchmark addresses in ZIP 98501. Refresh the government rate and address data for each new quarter using the import script. New Mexico uses its Taxation and Revenue Department's address and location-code service and requires a matching address record. The New Mexico and Hawaii seller levies support merchant absorption or an explicitly approved pass-through. Hawaii also requires a reviewed maximum pass-on rate. The ledger records the customer-funded portion separately within the seller levy.

For Maine, DC, four Hawaii counties, and the Vermont municipalities of Burlington, South Burlington and Williston, the preview can match the supplied street to Census geography and apply dated rate data. These initial rate records are limited through December 31, 2026. Other Vermont locations require their own reviewed records. The six illustrative destinations do not establish a customer’s tax rate. A reviewed exact-address record takes precedence. A full-address state match alone can support a reviewed no-collection or statewide exemption decision. It supplies no rate for a taxable sale. Missing jurisdiction rules still require review before actual orders can be accepted. All 50 states and DC start with shipping enabled.

Rate references include the [Maine rate table](https://www.maine.gov/revenue/taxes/sales-use-service-provider-tax/rates-due-dates), [DC rate notice](https://otr.cfo.dc.gov/release/notice-oct-1-2026-tax-changes), [Vermont local option guidance](https://tax.vermont.gov/business/local-option-tax), [Hawaii pass-on ceilings](https://tax.hawaii.gov/geninfo/countysurcharge/), [New Mexico address tool](https://www.tax.newmexico.gov/grt-rate-finder-tools/) and [Washington address lookup](https://dor.wa.gov/wa-sales-tax-rate-lookup-url-interface).

Complex local exemptions, differing local tax bases, price caps, line-level variations, separately taxable fees on exempt sales, seller-level deductions and graduated levies require additional implementation or review wherever applicable. ## Verification and production decision

The automated suite includes 80 tax tests. Of those, 51 check that each unapproved jurisdiction blocks calculation. Other cases cover the New York cent boundary, qualification and premium limits, New Jersey coins, mixed baskets and rounding, the Washington rate parser and sample, and missing data. A local PostgreSQL-compatible database exercises the migration, payment posting, repeat events, partial refunds, nexus and filing records. Boundary tests cover all five store floors and positive customer charges for the six example destinations. The full repository suite also checks checkout, payments, account, support and email behavior and a production build.

The test evidence covers implemented behavior. Comprehensive comparisons against independently verified legal examples for every state and locality, a full government data import, and an isolated hosted checkout-to-receipt test for each payment method are outstanding. The hosted public preview contains examples. The transactional tests used a local database. No production migrations, rules, registrations, payments or filings were applied. Production activation requires verified rates and product policies, business-specific registration and history, end-to-end payment tests on an isolated database, and the owner's rollout decision.
