## Six distinct stages
1. **Displayed spot reference:** indicative market information, not an executable offer or the final merchandise price. Dealer adjustments, premiums, taxes and shipping may differ from the reference.
2. **Temporary checkout quote:** a server-created, account-bound quote for specified goods, quantities, method and total. It expires ten minutes after server creation. A browser countdown cannot extend it. Creating a quote does not create an order or reserve a perpetual price.
3. **Submitted order:** your affirmative submission before quote expiration offers to buy the identified goods on the displayed terms. The receipt must identify its offer-expiry time. Proposed offer window: until the quoted ten-minute expiry; a later acceptance requires a new quote and new assent. You may withdraw an unaccepted offer through the order portal or support, subject to recording which event occurred first. No market-loss charge applies to a withdrawn, expired or rejected unaccepted offer.
4. **Company acceptance:** the Company issues a definitive notice titled “Order accepted — binding purchase agreement,” records its issuance, and makes it available in your account. The notice identifies the order, goods, quantity, total, agreement versions and payment deadline. It must match your still-open offer. A modified price or material term is a counteroffer requiring your new assent, not an acceptance.
5. **Binding purchase agreement:** acceptance binds both parties to the confirmed price and terms. Market movements alone do not entitle either party to cancel. This stage is distinct from payment clearance.
6. **Paid and verified order:** trusted payment confirmation and required review permit fulfillment. A payment screen, bank screenshot or submitted wire advice is not proof of cleared payment. Fraud review approval is not itself contractual acceptance.

## Price and payment
The server validates product availability, quantities, premiums, taxes, discounts, shipping and any permitted method charge. You cannot select a binding price by editing a request or browser state. Review the final itemized total and destination before submission. If a quote expires or changes, review and affirmatively accept a new one. A duplicate request must retrieve the same order, not create another obligation. No order may be accepted where applicable tax treatment or a permitted charge remains unresolved.

Only methods displayed as enabled are available. Cards and wires are proposed launch methods; cryptocurrency has a separate dormant draft. Checks are not authorized. Card authorization may temporarily reserve funds; capture and acceptance are separate recorded events. If funds are captured before rejection, the Company must arrange the appropriate refund or release rather than treating payment as irrevocable acceptance. A later failed payment does not silently rewrite an accepted contract.

## Performance and default
You agree to pay the confirmed amount by the method and deadline stated in the acceptance. We agree to supply the conforming goods under the accepted shipping promise, subject to lawful exceptions. Inform us promptly of a genuine banking or payment problem. After appropriate review and notice, an uncured breach may permit lawful cancellation and documented damages under the Market Loss Policy. No charge arises solely because the market moved, because we rejected an offer, or because we cannot fulfill it.

## Rejection, errors and interruptions
Before acceptance we may reject an order for inventory, pricing error, verification, sanctions, technical or payment reasons, subject to nondiscrimination and consumer law. After acceptance, an error or supplier issue does not confer an unlimited cancellation right. We will assess a legally valid basis, communicate the issue, and seek a consensual correction or provide the remedy required by law. We will not substitute goods without approval or cancel solely to obtain a better market price.

Genuine events outside reasonable control may delay performance only to the extent legally excused. We must reasonably mitigate, notify you, provide a revised shipping date where required, and obtain required delay consent or refund. Routine price changes or foreseeable supplier difficulties are not automatically force majeure. We may not keep funds indefinitely.

## Assent and records
The unchecked checkout acknowledgment identifies the linked documents, version and binding-order effect. Wire customers additionally acknowledge the disclosed deadline rule and its computed date. An acceptance record preserves your account, order, quote snapshot, exact presented text, version hashes, server time, payment method and deadline. The Company separately records acceptance issuance. Records are retrievable on request with identity verification. New terms do not apply to a prior accepted order without a legally valid, separately documented agreement.

Applicable refund rights, payment disputes, lawful defenses and mandatory consumer remedies remain available. You may ask for an itemized explanation of any disputed charge through support.
