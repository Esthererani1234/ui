## Scope and status
This proposed notice describes the reviewed website architecture and intended practices. Before publication the Company must verify its entity name, complete its vendor inventory and implement the retention and request procedures described below. It is not a claim that unverified controls or certifications already exist.

## Information we process
We process contact and account information such as names, email addresses and phone numbers; authentication and verification records; shipping and billing details; purchased products, prices, payment status and processor references; support messages, selling inquiries and photographs; and technical information such as IP-derived security information, device and browser information, page activity, referrers and approximate geography. Password authentication is handled through the authentication service; staff should not request passwords or full OTP values. Card entry is handled through Stripe’s payment components. The site stores payment metadata and references, not an intended copy of complete card credentials. Independent processor records are governed by the processor’s notice as well as applicable law.

Additional identity documentation may be requested for a specific lawful verification need. We do not routinely require government identification from every purchaser. Before collecting documents we will explain the purpose, provide a secure submission method, restrict access and establish retention. Do not upload identification, financial account credentials or unrelated people’s personal information to general support or selling photographs.

## Purposes and recipients
We use information to authenticate accounts, prepare and fulfill orders, communicate about payments and shipping, prevent fraud, secure the service, resolve requests and disputes, comply with law, maintain records and understand first-party site performance. Optional marketing requires separate choices and is not a condition of purchase.

The reviewed integrations include Supabase for authentication, database and private storage; Vercel for hosting and request handling; Stripe for card processing and payment risk controls; Bird/MessageBird and carriers for verification SMS; Resend for order and support email; and Cloudflare for the optional website assistant. Shipping carriers, any verified insurer, professional advisers and approved verification providers receive information necessary for their work. The precise carriers, insurers and any additional vendors must be confirmed before publication. We may disclose information pursuant to lawful process, to investigate fraud and protect rights, or in a business transfer subject to applicable safeguards. Payment and other providers may independently use data to meet their own legal obligations.

## Analytics and assistant
First-party analytics uses browser identifiers and sends activity to our server. The reviewed implementation observes Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track by disabling those analytics and provides a browser opt-out. That control does not prevent essential security or order processing. No advertising-pixel inventory has been certified by this draft. We do not claim that information is never disclosed, or that every disclosure is exempt from statutory “sale” or “sharing” definitions; counsel must classify the actual vendor arrangements.

If you use the optional assistant, your message and recent conversation context may be sent to Cloudflare to generate an answer. Do not include secrets or sensitive documents. The assistant can make errors and cannot approve contractual changes, refunds or account decisions. Use human support for those matters.

## Retention and security
We retain information as reasonably necessary for the identified purposes, applicable law, unresolved transactions, fraud investigations, disputes and legal holds. A request to delete an account does not immediately erase all transaction or legal records. Access restrictions, private storage and server checks are part of the reviewed architecture, but no service can promise absolute security. We will investigate suspected incidents and provide legally required notices. Specific retention periods and backup expiry will be documented internally and verified before this notice takes effect.

## Requests and choices
Contact support@goldonthespot.com with “Privacy request,” or use the approved account request channel when available. Describe whether you seek access, correction, deletion, a copy, an opt-out or an appeal; do not include a password or code. We will reasonably verify identity and authority proportionately to the request without collecting unnecessary identification. Where applicable, an authorized agent may act with proof of authority. We will respond within the applicable legal period and explain lawful limitations, extensions and available appeal procedures. We will not unlawfully discriminate for exercising privacy rights. State rights and applicability vary; the Privacy Rights Notice supplements this policy.

The service is for adults and is not directed to children under 13. Notify us of suspected child data so we can investigate and take appropriate action. United States operations do not mean every provider processes data only in the United States. Material practice changes require updated notice and any legally required consent; new notice does not authorize incompatible retroactive uses.
