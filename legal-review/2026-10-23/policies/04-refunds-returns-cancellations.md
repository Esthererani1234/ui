## Conspicuous restriction on discretionary cancellations
**After definitive Company acceptance, bullion purchases are not ordinarily cancelable or returnable for buyer’s remorse, market movements, finding a lower price or changed investment preferences.** The restriction does not apply merely because you opened a quote or received a submission receipt. You may withdraw an unaccepted offer before acceptance. Do not assume a support request has canceled an already accepted order; request written confirmation.

## Rights that remain
This policy preserves applicable remedies for non-delivery, incorrect merchandise, materially inaccurate purity, weight or description, counterfeit products, misrepresentation, seller breach, unauthorized payments and other legally required refunds. It does not waive statutory warranties or card-dispute rights. If we cannot fulfill an accepted order, we will provide the legally required remedy rather than impose a cancellation fee on you.

## Reporting a problem
Contact support with your order number and a description. We prefer notice of visible shipping or merchandise problems within 48 hours of receipt so evidence can be preserved. **This is a preferred reporting period, not a forfeiture of rights after 48 hours.** Preserve packaging and available photographs; do not endanger yourself, disclose sensitive information publicly, or alter the metal while a claim is assessed. A failure to make an unboxing video does not by itself defeat a claim.

## Returns and remedies
Request return instructions before sending valuable goods. We will specify a secure return destination and appropriate shipping method. For substantiated seller error or defective/nonconforming goods we will bear reasonable authorized return costs to the extent required by law and provide the applicable repair, replacement, refund or other remedy. We will not insist on an unwanted substitute where you are legally entitled to a refund. A return inspection must be prompt and must not be used to postpone a mandatory refund unlawfully.

## Refund amounts and timing
Approved refunds normally go to the original method, subject to lawful verification and processor constraints. We will explain the amount and expected processing time and initiate refunds within the applicable legal deadline. Bank posting time is separate from initiation. A mandatory refund will not be reduced by an unsupported processing, restocking or market-loss fee. We will correct applicable tax and shipping amounts as law requires. No new discretionary restocking fee is proposed for launch.

A discretionary release from an accepted contract may involve separately explained, legally recoverable default damages under the Market Loss Policy; it is not permission to deduct an arbitrary amount from funds we hold. We will itemize any proposed offset, assess its legal basis and allow review. No default authorizes confiscation of the entire payment. A chargeback does not automatically establish customer fraud, and a refund does not authorize duplicate recovery. Disputed provider transactions will be reconciled so neither party receives an improper double payment.
