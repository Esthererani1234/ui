## Sending payment safely
Use the beneficiary and bank details in the signed-in order record after acceptance and the corresponding authenticated notice. Include the exact order reference. Do not send funds to a changed destination based solely on an email, text, assistant response or unsolicited call. Stop and verify conflicting instructions through the known website contact, using a channel independent of the suspicious message. The Company will not announce a bank-account change by an unverified message alone.

A domestic USD wire ordinarily uses the bank’s approved domestic routing instructions. Use SWIFT or intermediary details only if the receiving bank actually requires and has approved them. No bank number or beneficiary is supplied by this draft. The account must belong to an approved beneficiary consistent with the contracting entity. Bank data must not be public website content or exposed to another customer.

## Amount, timing and verification
Arrange for the full confirmed amount to arrive, accounting for your bank’s cutoff and charges. A submitted transfer confirmation is not cleared funds. Tell us promptly about a genuine delay and provide a reference through support; do not send a duplicate wire while the first is being traced. We will assess banking disruptions reasonably and may document an extension without changing the agreed merchandise price. A bank processing delay is not automatically fraud or intentional misconduct.

Partial payment does not authorize partial shipment or amend the agreement. We will identify an underpayment and request an agreed resolution. Overpayments are reconciled and, where return is required, returned through a verified lawful channel; they are not a gratuity. Third-party payments require advance review and may be refused. We may investigate mismatched names, recalled transfers or suspicious confirmations, but will explain the disposition as law permits.

## Late payment and default
Contact us before sending after the deadline or cancellation. A late payment does not automatically revive a canceled order or create a new price lock. We will reconcile it and either agree a lawful resolution or return the amount due, subject to sanctions or other legal restraints. We will not keep late funds indefinitely. Before claiming default loss, we will establish acceptance, the disclosed deadline, nonperformance and any appropriate extension or excuse, and follow the Market Loss Policy. No automatic fixed fee, forfeiture or stored-card charge is authorized.

Fulfillment requires verified funds, acceptance and required security checks. We will not let an unexplained review hold displace applicable shipping, delay-consent or refund obligations.
