## Authorized payment and itemized total
Use only a card you are authorized to use. The final total must be calculated by the server and displayed before authorization. Stripe processes card entry and may require authentication or additional checks. Do not send full card numbers or security codes to our support staff. A failed attempt must not create duplicate obligations; contact support if an attempt appears uncertain before paying again.

## Authorization, capture, acceptance and fulfillment
An authorization is a temporary issuer hold, not settlement or Company acceptance. Capture requests payment; settlement and fraud review are separate processes. The Company’s definitive acceptance notice creates the contract as described in the Purchase Agreement. A browser success page is not proof that payment succeeded. Fulfillment requires authenticated server confirmation of the correct order, currency and amount, contractual acceptance and required review.

If an offer is rejected, we will release an authorization or initiate the appropriate refund for captured funds. Reversals, disputes and partial or full refunds must remain visible in your order history even if a package was previously delivered. A payment dispute does not erase the shipment history, and shipment does not erase the refund history.

## Surcharges — release blocker
No new surcharge is authorized by this draft. Before any revised card pricing is published, the Company must obtain processor approval and comply with applicable state and network rules, including total-price disclosures, caps, receipt treatment and restrictions for debit and prepaid cards. A generic card fee cannot be assumed permissible for debit cards. The presently configured 4% fee must be resolved before launch; this draft does not validate it or silently amend an existing order. Counsel and the processor must approve either compliant method-specific handling or a pricing approach that avoids a surcharge. Calling an added fee a “discount” does not by itself make it compliant.

## Disputes and refunds
You retain statutory and network rights to dispute unauthorized, undelivered, incorrect or otherwise qualifying transactions. Contacting us can help resolve an issue but is not a prerequisite where law or issuer rules allow direct action. We may submit accurate order, assent, payment and delivery records in response to a dispute. We will not misrepresent a legitimate dispute as fraud or impose an automatic chargeback penalty. Refunds are ordinarily made to the original method under the Refund Policy. We will reconcile refunds and disputes to prevent duplicate recovery, without withholding a lawful refund merely because processor fees are nonrefundable to us.

This agreement does not authorize a later off-session card charge for market loss, collection fees or other alleged damages. Such payment would require a separate legally sufficient authorization and processor permission.
