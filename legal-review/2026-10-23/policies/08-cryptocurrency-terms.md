## Not available at launch under the reviewed configuration
Cryptocurrency is disabled in the reviewed payment configuration. This document is a preparation draft, not an offer to accept cryptocurrency or an instruction to send it. No tokens, networks, wallet addresses or confirmation counts are approved here. Do not send cryptocurrency through support, social messages or an unsolicited address.

## Preconditions to activation
Before activation, the Company must identify and approve its processor, supported tokens and exact networks, settlement currency, screening procedures, confirmation policy, quote duration, underpayment tolerance, wrong-network recovery process, refund method and any fees. These details must be displayed before assent, reflected in the server invoice and tested in an isolated environment. Counsel must assess sanctions, money-transmission and New York licensing issues in light of actual activity; merchant payment acceptance does not authorize exchange, custody or transmission services.

## Proposed transaction rules
Only the exact token, network, destination and amount on a current server-issued invoice may be used. Blockchain transaction submission is not settlement; the processor’s authenticated confirmation and amount matching govern payment verification. Network congestion can delay confirmation. An expired invoice cannot be revived by changing a browser clock. An order may be accepted only under the Purchase Agreement; funds received on an expired or rejected order require reconciliation rather than automatic fulfillment at an old price.

Underpayments do not authorize shipment. Overpayments and duplicate payments require reconciliation and return where required. We will provide a controlled resolution process for a payment received after expiry. Wrong-token or wrong-network transfers may be technically unrecoverable, but we will not promise recovery or automatically retain recoverable value without a lawful basis. Any optional recovery service and actual charge must be separately disclosed and agreed before work. No recovery fee may be deducted from a legally mandatory refund unless lawful.

## Proposed refund basis — requires approval
The proposed commercial basis is the accepted order’s USD amount refundable under the Refund Policy, with the refund token quantity calculated using the approved processor’s disclosed rate at refund execution. This may differ from the original token quantity. The final method, rate source and necessary destination verification must be disclosed before payment; it cannot be selected opportunistically after a dispute. Mandatory law may require a different result. Refunds must not be directed to an unrelated third-party wallet without lawful verification. A legally blocked transaction must be handled under sanctions requirements, not reflexively returned.

Cryptocurrency transfers may lack card-like reversal mechanisms and involve exchange-rate, network, address, fraud and regulatory risks. This does not waive seller obligations or mandatory customer remedies. This draft grants no permission to automatically accept cryptocurrency.
