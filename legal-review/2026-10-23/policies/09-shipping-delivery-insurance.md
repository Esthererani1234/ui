## Destination, charges and proposed dispatch promise
We package and dispatch accepted orders from New York City to approved United States destinations. Available delivery addresses and charges must be stated before order submission. Freight forwarding, rerouting and other unusual destinations require review; no international shipping is promised.

The proposed ordinary dispatch window is **three to seven business days after verified payment clearance and necessary security checks**. Dispatch means handoff to the carrier, not arrival. Before publication, the Company must establish a reasonable basis for this promise and show an actual ship-by date or a legally sufficient estimate at ordering. Internal acceptance or review cannot postpone the statutory shipping clock indefinitely. Where the FTC rule measures time from a properly completed order, that rule controls over this proposed wording. No order should be taken on a promise the Company cannot reasonably meet.

## Delays
If we cannot ship within the applicable promise, we will notify you before it expires, explain the delay, provide a revised date when supported, and offer the cancellation/refund choices required by law. We will obtain affirmative delay consent when required; silence is not treated as blanket consent. Further or indefinite delays require the applicable renewed notice and consent or refund. Payment or supplier outages do not authorize indefinite retention of funds.

## Carrier, signature and insurance
Carrier and signature arrangements will reflect the order value, destination and verified coverage. Tracking will be available when assigned. **No blanket promise of full insurance is approved by this draft.** The Company must verify that any policy covers bullion, the actual carrier, each shipment value, signature method, packaging and relevant exclusions before advertising coverage or dispatching an order that depends on it. Carrier declared value alone must not be represented as a verified insurance policy.

For the proposed consumer delivery contract, the Company remains responsible for conforming delivery to the agreed destination, subject to applicable law and particular facts; risk is not transferred merely by handing the parcel to a carrier. A scan or signature is evidence, not conclusive proof against a substantiated incorrect-delivery claim. Customer-authorized address changes, unattended delivery or signature waivers may affect a claim according to causation, coverage and law, but do not automatically extinguish all rights. Obtain approval before requesting such changes.

## Missing, damaged or incorrect delivery
Contact support promptly, preferably within 48 hours for visible problems. Keep packaging and relevant photographs when safe. We may reasonably request a factual statement and cooperation with carrier or insurer investigations. Do not return valuable goods without secure instructions. If a delivery appears damaged or suspicious, request that the carrier record its condition where feasible; accepting a package does not automatically waive rights.

We will investigate without unreasonable delay. A legally required refund or replacement does not depend on the insurer first paying us. Claims must be assessed on evidence, not automatically denied for lack of an unboxing video or a late preferred notice. Refused or unclaimed packages require reconciliation and a lawful agreed disposition; they are not automatically forfeited or sold for our benefit. Reasonable re-shipping charges require a lawful basis and disclosure.
