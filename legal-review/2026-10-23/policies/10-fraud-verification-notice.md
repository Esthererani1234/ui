## Purpose and proportionate checks
Precious-metal orders may involve significant value. We use account verification, payment and address checks, order history and appropriate security signals to reduce unauthorized transactions and meet applicable obligations. Email or SMS verification alone is not a government identity check or a guarantee of ownership. A shared telephone number or unusual order is a reason for review, not an automatic accusation of fraud.

Additional information may be requested when reasonably necessary for an identified risk or legal obligation, including mismatched purchaser/payment information, unusually large or repeated transactions, disputed payments or sanctions concerns. We do not require a government ID from every ordinary customer by default. We will describe the requested information and secure submission method and limit access and retention. Do not place sensitive documents in general inquiry photographs or email.

## Holds and outcomes
Before acceptance we may pause or reject an offer while assessing risk. A hold must be labeled clearly and must not falsely state that payment has cleared or the order is accepted. After acceptance, any hold, cancellation or refund must have a lawful contractual basis and preserve shipping-delay and refund rights. We will avoid disclosing details that would help circumvent safeguards, while providing an appropriate status and contact procedure.

We may screen parties and transactions against applicable sanctions requirements and review third-party payments. A potential match requires competent human assessment; it is not proof of wrongdoing. Where law requires blocked property to remain blocked, we may be unable to return it without authorization. Otherwise, investigation does not give us an unlimited right to keep funds. Identity documents should be deleted or minimized when the justified retention period ends, subject to lawful holds.

## Compliance limitations
This notice does not claim a certified AML program, government approval, universal KYC completion or a sanctions service already operating. The Company must determine the applicability of FinCEN dealer obligations using its actual purchase sources, covered goods and sales, and implement any required program before relying on this notice. Inquiry-only Sell to Us does not authorize purchases from the public, refining or cash transactions. Report suspected account or payment misuse through the known website support contact. We will preserve relevant evidence with access controls and avoid logging passwords, codes or full payment credentials.
