## Price and resale risk
Physical precious-metal prices fluctuate and can fall substantially. You may lose money. Past performance and historical scarcity do not guarantee future returns or purchasing power. A product’s retail price can include manufacturing, distribution, dealer premiums, taxes, payment costs and shipping in addition to metal value. Its resale value may be below both your purchase price and a displayed spot reference.

A price lock fixes a particular transaction price for the disclosed period or accepted contract; it does not insure against loss or promise a future repurchase price. Dealer buy and sell prices differ. Numismatic condition, brand, packaging, purity, fine metal content, market demand and product availability may affect price independently of spot. Gross weight is not always the amount of pure metal: alloyed coins may contain a specified fine-metal weight within a greater gross weight.
