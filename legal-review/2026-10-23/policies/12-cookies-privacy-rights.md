## Browser technologies
The website uses local storage, session storage, cookies or similar technology for account sessions, cart continuity, security, payment interactions and optional first-party analytics. “Cookie” here includes comparable identifiers; turning off only browser cookies may not clear local storage. Supabase client authentication currently uses browser storage. This draft does not claim all session material is protected by HttpOnly cookies.

The reviewed analytics uses a persistent visitor identifier and a session identifier. Its opt-out removes those identifiers and suppresses queued analytics, and it observes Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track for this first-party tracking. Essential account, payment and security operations continue where necessary. Clearing storage may sign you out, remove cart state and reset a locally saved preference. If you use several browsers, set preferences on each unless an account-wide control is provided. Provider-hosted payment pages may have separate necessary technologies and notices.

## State privacy rights
Depending on your state, the applicable law and whether it covers this business, you may have rights to know or access information, obtain a copy, correct inaccuracies, delete information, opt out of qualifying sale, targeted advertising or certain profiling, limit certain sensitive uses, use an authorized agent, appeal a decision, and receive nondiscriminatory treatment. These rights are subject to lawful identity verification and exceptions, including necessary transaction and legal records. This notice does not assert that every comprehensive state law applies to this Company or that every state grants identical rights.

Send a request to support@goldonthespot.com with “Privacy request.” We will record the applicable jurisdiction and statutory response deadline, request only proportionate verification, and explain any refusal, extension and applicable appeal route. If an appeal is available, reply with “Privacy appeal” and the request reference for a separate review. Regulatory complaint rights are preserved. A verified account-deletion request can close access while legally retained order records remain restricted. We will identify categories retained and their reasons rather than promise immediate erasure of everything, including backups.

Before publication, the owner must approve the request channel, staff responsibility, vendor deletion process and retention schedule, and counsel must assess state-law thresholds using actual revenue, processing volumes and business activities.
