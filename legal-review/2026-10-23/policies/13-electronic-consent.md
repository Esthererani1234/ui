## Scope of proposed electronic delivery
Subject to the disclosures below and your affirmative choice, we propose providing order offers, definitive acceptances, payment instructions, receipts, shipping updates, policy versions and transaction records electronically through your account and email. An intentional affirmative checkbox or equivalent signature can evidence assent to the identified agreement. Merely sending an email address or browsing does not sign a purchase agreement.

Consent to electronically delivered legally required written disclosures must be obtained separately where law requires it. It is not identical to agreement to buy, marketing consent or permission to debit a payment method. Any legally required paper notice or delivery method remains available as applicable.

## Access requirements and consent procedure
You need a supported browser with internet access, a functioning email account, the ability to view ordinary HTML and, for PDF attachments, a PDF reader, and a way to download, print or otherwise retain records. Before requesting consent, the implementation must display a sample record in the format actually used and require an affirmative action that reasonably demonstrates access. A browser checkbox alone must not be represented as sufficient for every E-SIGN consumer disclosure requirement.

The proposed acknowledgment is: “I consent to receive the listed transaction records electronically. I have opened the sample record and can download or print it. I understand how to request paper copies, update my contact information and withdraw electronic-delivery consent.” It begins unchecked. Keep a copy of this disclosure and the transaction documents.

## Paper copies, withdrawal and changes
Request a paper copy or withdraw electronic-delivery consent through support using the transaction or account reference. Proposed paper-copy charge: none. The verified mailing address for requests must be supplied before launch. Withdrawal is effective prospectively after reasonable processing and does not invalidate previously delivered records or existing obligations. We will explain any practical effect on future online transactions and provide legally required notices through an appropriate alternative; we will not cancel vested customer remedies as a penalty for withdrawal.

Update your email and mailing address through the verified account procedure. A failed email delivery requires a reasonable follow-up method for material notices, not a fiction that you necessarily read it. If hardware or software changes create a material risk that you cannot access or retain required records, we will provide updated requirements and obtain any new consent required by law. Marketing withdrawal is handled separately and is not treated as withdrawal from an existing purchase agreement.

Records must remain accessible in reproducible form for the applicable retention period, with the exact version you accepted. No later policy edit silently replaces your archived contract.
