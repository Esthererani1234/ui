## Proposed informal process
Either party may send a written dispute notice describing the transaction, facts, requested relief and contact details to the other’s verified contact, including the Company notice address and support email. The parties will try in good faith to resolve it for 30 days after receipt. This process does not prevent timely small-claims filing, urgent provisional relief, regulatory complaints, payment disputes or exercise of rights whose deadlines would otherwise expire. The Company agrees not to assert expiration caused solely by its agreed informal period, to the extent lawful; do not assume a private agreement extends every statutory deadline.

## Proposed arbitration scope and choice
If separately and validly accepted and not timely opted out, covered disputes arising from the identified future purchase or website relationship would be resolved individually by one neutral arbitrator under the AAA Consumer Arbitration Rules then applicable, subject to this agreement and mandatory law. The Federal Arbitration Act governs where it applies; otherwise controlling state law applies. A court decides whether an agreement was formed and whether this provision is enforceable; no broad delegation of those formation questions is proposed.

Either party may pursue an eligible individual small-claims case in the appropriate court. Claims excluded from predispute arbitration by statute remain excluded. Either party may seek necessary temporary court relief without surrendering the ultimate merits process. Consumers may communicate with regulators and exercise issuer dispute rights. Arbitration is not a complete loss of the right to go to court.

## Fair process and costs
The hearing will be remote, by documents, or in a reasonably convenient location consistent with the consumer rules and the consumer’s lawful rights. Each party may present evidence and request appropriate disclosure. The arbitrator may award available individual statutory and equitable remedies and must provide a reasoned written decision. Judicial review and enforcement rights provided by law are preserved.

The Company will pay administrator and arbitrator costs assigned to it by applicable consumer rules and any additional amount needed to avoid an unlawful or prohibitively expensive consumer process. Consumer fees will not exceed the amount permitted by those rules and applicable law, with available hardship relief. Each side bears its own attorney costs except where statute or a lawful award permits shifting; this clause is not automatic loser-pays. If AAA cannot administer, the parties may agree a reputable alternative; absent agreement, the dispute may proceed in a court with jurisdiction rather than being trapped without a forum.

## Proposed class waiver, severability and opt-out
Where enforceable, covered arbitration proceeds individually, not as a class or representative proceeding. This does not waive a nonwaivable remedy or public-injunctive right. If a class waiver cannot lawfully be enforced for a claim, that claim proceeds in court rather than compelling class arbitration by implication. Other severance must preserve fair access and required remedies.

You may opt out of this proposed arbitration agreement within 30 calendar days after first accepting it by sending your name, account email, order reference if any, and a clear opt-out statement to the verified support or mailing address. No explanation or payment is required; no purchase penalty applies. Keep confirmation. Opt-out affects arbitration and the associated class waiver, not the rest of a valid purchase agreement. A new version cannot erase a prior valid opt-out or apply retroactively to accrued disputes without a lawful separate agreement.

Before release the Company must register the clause where required by the administrator, provide conspicuous separate assent, test opt-out recording and obtain licensed attorney sign-off. The administrator’s review is not government or judicial approval.
