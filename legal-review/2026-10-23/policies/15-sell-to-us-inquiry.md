## Inquiry only
The Selling Center lets an authenticated customer ask about potentially selling gold, silver, platinum, palladium, coins, bars, scrap, broken jewelry or other materials the Company may consider. A case number, message, photograph, WhatsApp conversation, estimate or preliminary response is not a binding purchase contract, a guaranteed payout, acceptance of goods or permission to ship material.

Describe the material accurately and indicate what you know and what is uncertain. Photographs alone cannot establish purity, fine weight, authenticity or ownership. Do not submit stolen material, false ownership information or another person’s private documents. You retain ownership of your submitted content while allowing us to use it to evaluate and respond to your inquiry under the Privacy Policy. This is not permission to use your photographs in advertising.

## Possible later process
If the Company is willing and legally authorized to proceed, a separate written agreement must define material, ownership assurances, shipment and risk, custody, inspection, testing, pricing basis, fees, acceptance, rejection, return and payout. Any assay, destructive test, melting or refining requires specific prior written authorization and an approved lawful process. No inquiry authorizes us to melt your property. Final value depends on verified material, approved evaluation and the separately agreed pricing basis; an estimate is not a final offer.

## No unsolicited shipments or automatic buying
Do not mail or deliver metal until you receive specific authorized instructions and agree the separate terms. The Company is not offering an automated public purchasing or refining program at launch. Before expanding into purchases, it must assess licensing, recordkeeping, AML, sanctions, custody and insurance obligations, including any applicable New York City secondhand-dealer rules and exemptions. This inquiry service does not represent that such licenses or arrangements exist.

Use the separate Selling Center case for related questions and status. Support tickets handle unrelated shopping or account issues. We may decline an inquiry without creating a purchase obligation, subject to applicable law. Inquiry data and photographs are handled under the Privacy Policy, with account verification for access. No party loses mandatory rights merely by using the inquiry portal.
